---
title: least pre-fixed point
tags:
  - definition
---
a pre-fixed point of a function $f: D \rightarrow D$, where $D$ is a [[notes/partially ordered set|poset]], is some $d\in D$ such that $f(d) \sqsubseteq d$.

the least pre-fixed point $\text{fix}(f)$ is the least such point. that is: $\forall d \in D$, if $d$ is a pre-fixed point then $\text{fix}(f) \sqsubseteq d$. 

notably, $\text{fix}(f)$ is also the least *fixed point* of $f$, since all fixed points are also pre-fixed points.
