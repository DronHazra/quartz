> In computing and communication systems, a **work-conserving scheduler** is a [scheduler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheduler "Scheduler") that always tries to keep the scheduled resource(s) busy, if there are submitted jobs ready to be scheduled. In contrast, a **non-work conserving scheduler** is a scheduler that, in some cases, may leave the scheduled resource(s) [idle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idle_(CPU) "Idle (CPU)") despite the presence of jobs ready to be scheduled.
