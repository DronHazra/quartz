## things i've done

Most of my experience is in ML research, in and around AI safety and robustness. I've worked on:
- Generative world modeling for inferring 3-D scenes from 2-D images at the [Center for Human-Compatible AI](https://humancompatible.ai/) with [Shreyas Kapur](https://shreyaskapur.com/) (Berkeley)
- Scalably testing worst-case adversarial robustness in computer vision with [Dan Hendrycks](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~hendrycks/) (Center for AI Safety) and [Max Kaufmann](https://maxkaufmann.com/) (UK AI Safety Institute)
- Investigating universality of Sparse Autoencoder bases with [Davis Brown](https://davisrbrown.com/) (UPenn)

In the past, I also worked with distributed systems at [Zilliqa](https://www.zilliqa.com/) and did some [weird stuff](https://dronhazra.com/posts/pizza-prototypes) at [X](https://x.company/). I have a somewhat amateur fascination with generative models for music, and I used to be mediocre at math olympiads, back when I got the median score at the Canadian Math Olympiad.
