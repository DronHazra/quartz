[[chebyshev inequality]]

in general, given $f:\mathbb{R} \rightarrow [0, \infty)$ and increasing, then $$P(X\ge a) \le P \left(f(X) \ge f(a)\right) \le \frac{E(f(X))}{f(a)}$$ and similarly for decreasing with the signs reversed.
