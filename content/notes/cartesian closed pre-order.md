---
title: cartesian closed pre-order
tags:
  - definition
---
a preorder (i.e. zero or one arrows between objects), with:
- binary products (which we write as $A\wedge B$)
- binary coproducts (which we write $A \vee B$)
- exponential objects (which we rate $A \rightarrow B$)
- a least element $\bot$
- a greatest element $\top$
